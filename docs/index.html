Track record (paper trading)

How each paper bet works: a bracket becomes a bet the first moment its edge (model probability − market price) reaches ±0.08 — that snapshot's price is the entry price, shown as "bought" below. Each qualifying bracket buys exactly one hypothetical $1-payout share sized at that price — no bigger stakes for bigger edges, no shared bankroll, no cap on how many can fire at once. There's no total "amount invested" concept: every qualifying bracket is its own independent $1-notional position. The position is held to resolution with no early exit — if the price swings against it before the market closes, that isn't currently acted on (see below).
